Direct store delivery, from presell to invoice

Order management built for distributors who deliver every day

OrderPulse is direct store delivery software that takes the order in the field or the office, applies the right promotions, reserves stock and turns the order into an invoice, in one flow.

Four tools, one order flow

Every tool works on the same customers, products and stock, so an order is never typed twice.

Mobile

Sales app

  • Check in at the customer and start the visit
  • Take orders online or offline
  • Add lines with unit, quantity, price and promotion
  • Review the totals and submit
See it
Web

Web back office

  • Create orders as drafts, then confirm them
  • Total, line count and invoice status on every order
  • Reservation status on every line
  • Warnings when stock is short
See it
Pricing

Promotions engine

  • Rules such as buy two get one free or a percentage off
  • Eligible promotions listed once the customer is chosen
  • Applied automatically, line by line
  • The order shows what was applied and what was not
See it
Billing

Invoicing

  • Convert an order to an invoice from its actions menu
  • Customer, order, warehouse and lines come across prefilled
  • Promotions already on the order carry over
  • Choose the payment terms and create the invoice

The whole operation on one screen

An order shows its status, its totals and the reservation on each line, with the next step one click away.

  • 1

    Status at a glance

    Order total, line count and invoice status sit at the top of the order.

  • 2

    Stock reserved per line

  • 3

    Straight to the invoice

    Convert to invoice is in the same actions menu as the pick list.

Built for the route

The OrderPulse mobile app puts the customer, their history and the order form in the rep's hand, online or offline.

  • Check-in

    Check in at the customer and the visit timer starts.

  • New order

    Start a sales order for the customer you are visiting.

  • Promotions on the line

    Each line shows the promotion that applies and the unit price.

  • Review & submit

    Check subtotal, discounts and total, then submit.

  • Collect payment

    Take a payment from the customer card.

  • Returns & credits

    Record a return or a credit from the same card.
