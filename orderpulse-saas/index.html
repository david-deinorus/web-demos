How it works

From the first order line to the invoice

Sales orders

Create the order once. Everything else follows from it.

Pick the customer and the warehouse, add the lines and save. The order stays a draft until someone confirms it.

  • Customer, warehouse and order date on a single screen
  • Draft, review, then confirm when it is ready
  • Total, line count and invoice status shown on the order
Learn more
Promotions

Promotions applied automatically

Define a rule once under Pricing & Promotions. From then on it is applied to the order lines that qualify.

  • Rules such as buy two get one free or a percentage off
  • Eligible promotions appear as soon as the customer is chosen
  • The order shows what was applied and what was not

Inventory

Stock reserved on confirm

Confirming an order puts its stock aside right away, and the order moves straight to Allocated.

  • Reservation happens automatically on confirm
  • Every line shows its own reservation status
  • If stock is short, you are told by how much and can reserve later

FAQ

Questions distributors ask

What is direct store delivery (DSD) software?

It is the software behind selling to stores and delivering to them from your own trucks. It covers taking the order, applying prices and promotions, holding stock, running the route and invoicing. OrderPulse brings those steps into one platform.

Can reps take orders without a connection?

Yes. Orders can be taken in the field or the office, online or offline, from the OrderPulse mobile app.

How do promotions work?

You define the rules once, for example buy two and get one free, or a percentage off. When you pick a customer, the promotions available to them are listed, and the ones that qualify are applied to the order lines. The order shows what was applied.

When is stock reserved?

When the order is confirmed. If there is enough stock, each line shows as fully reserved and the order is allocated. If not, you are told how many units are missing and can reserve later.

Which systems does OrderPulse connect with?

QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop, NetSuite, Sage 50 and Odoo for accounting and ERP; Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce for online stores; FedEx and UPS for shipping; and EDI with Kroger, SPS Commerce and TrueCommerce. Tell us what you run and we will go through it on the demo.

How do I get pricing and get started?

Book a demo and we will cover your operation and pricing together. After that we set up your warehouses, products and customers and import your data, and then your team goes live.
