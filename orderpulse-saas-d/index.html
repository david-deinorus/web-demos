How an order moves through OrderPulse

Each step picks up where the last one left off.

  1. Presell

    Reps capture the order in the field or the office.

  2. Order

    Promotions apply line by line. The order stays a draft until confirmed.

  3. Reserve

    Confirming sets the stock aside and the order moves to Allocated.

  4. Invoice

    Convert it, and the invoice opens with customer, lines and promotions filled in.
