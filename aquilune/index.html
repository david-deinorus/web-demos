I — Arrival

At the end of the last road, the coast folds in on itself.

Aquilune is twelve rooms stacked down a limestone hillside, all facing west. Nobody built it in a hurry, and nobody will ask you to leave in one.

II — Dusk

When the sun goes, the house wakes.

Lamps are lit by hand, one window at a time. Dinner is a single long table: one menu, whatever the boats brought in, and somebody to pour.

III — Night

Then the water answers.

Some nights the cove glows, a living blue that follows a hand, an oar, a swimmer. We call them glow nights. You can book them.
